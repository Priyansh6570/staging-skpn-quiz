8. CAVEATS ON SECTIONS 5 AND 6
   The 34% similarity cut-off is arbitrary. Where an item in section 5 sits next to an
   unclaimed lib/i18n entry of almost the same name, it is very likely the same art and
   only just failed the cut-off:
     book 13 चित्राश्ययोगा  ~  lib/i18n 14 चित्रयोग (Chitra-yoga) at 38%
     book 50 यन्त्रमात्रिका  ~  lib/i18n 52 यंत्रमातृका (Yantra-matrika) at 36%
     book 61 बालकड़नक  ~  lib/i18n 62 बालक्रीडनक (Balakridanaka) at 40%
   Matching is greedy, so where the book SPLITS one canonical compound into two numbers
   the first number claims the canonical entry and the second falls into section 5.
   Cross-read section 5 against section 3 before concluding anything is missing.
